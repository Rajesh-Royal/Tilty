Big buttons for tiling windows.

Tilty puts Fill, Left half and Right half in the title bar of the window you're using. One click with the trackpad and it's done. No hovering over the green button, no waiting, no shortcuts to remember.

Made for one laptop and one trackpad.

If I had a keyboard and a mouse, I would not have built this.

I use a laptop only, and I use the trackpad only. My fingers are big and the macOS window buttons are small. Keyboard shortcuts are hard to remember.

To make a window bigger without going full screen, I had to hover over the green button, wait for the menu, and then pick Fill. One slip and the window went full screen instead.

I don't want to wait even a second. It should be click and done. That's Tilty.

Three buttons. That's the whole app.

  • Fill

    Makes the window as big as the screen without going full screen. The menu bar and Dock stay right where they are.

  • Left half

    Snaps the window to the left side of the screen it's on.

  • Right half

    Snaps the window to the right side. Click the same button again and the window goes back to where it was.

The buttons follow whichever window you're using, hide while you drag, and stay out of the way in full screen. In Chrome they sit at the right end of the tab strip so they never cover a tab.

Install it in a minute.

  1. Download Tilty.dmg, open it and drag Tilty into your Applications folder.

  2. Tilty isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Open Tilty and click Done on the warning. Then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Tilty.

  3. Tilty asks for this so it can move other apps' windows. Click Open System Settings and turn on Tilty. Now click into any window and the buttons are right there, next to the traffic lights.
